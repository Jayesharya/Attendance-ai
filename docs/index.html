About this walkthrough

What this demo shows — and what it doesn’t.

This static GitHub Pages page generates sample data locally in your browser. It does not upload classroom media or share student information.

Motion signal

A generated room-level movement pattern. It cannot tell whether a learner is focused or distracted.

Speech-like audio

A generated probability pattern, not recorded sound, a transcript, or speaker label.

Presence estimates

Anonymous sample counts, not named students. Verify attendance against an official roster.

Teaching prompts

Simple rules-based ideas. A teacher decides what fits the lesson and learners.

Local app

The repository also has a Python app for local webcam/video and optional room-audio analysis.

Responsible use

Never use activity signals for grading, discipline, diagnosis, or student ranking. Follow notice and consent requirements.
