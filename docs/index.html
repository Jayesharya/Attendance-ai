A note on interpretation

What this walkthrough can show

A small interface prototype for reviewing broad, synthetic classroom patterns.

Room-level signals

Movement and sound patterns are illustrative summaries. They cannot tell you whether someone is focused, confused, or learning.

Presence estimates

Counts can be inaccurate in real settings. Confirm attendance with the approved class process.

Teacher judgment

Prompts are suggestions for reflection. They do not evaluate teaching or individual students.

Privacy boundary

This public page runs on generated data only. It has no media capture, upload, sign-in, or data submission.
